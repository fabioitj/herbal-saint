# Generated botanical assets

These are generated concept visuals for the Herbal Saint website, not photographs of a verified physical product or garden. Both images were created with the built-in imagegen tool and visually reviewed. The original PNG outputs were converted to WebP using Sharp at quality 88 and effort 6; their dimensions were preserved.

## Hero apothecary: final generation prompt

```text
Use case: product-mockup
Asset type: original premium botanical brand website hero photograph, landscape 3:2 image
Primary request: a premium editorial apothecary still life for HERBAL SAINT.
Scene/backdrop: warm bone cream studio wall approximately #ebe8de and pale limestone surface; a single low weathered pale limestone plinth.
Subject: one dark amber glass cylindrical apothecary bottle with a simple matte ivory paper label and a dark olive cap, nestled among sculptural fresh green melissa and mint stems with beautifully veined textured leaves, and a few small real white chamomile flowers with yellow centers.
Style/medium: photorealistic luxury botanical editorial product photography; tactile organic real-world textures, magazine quality, not a 3D render.
Composition/framing: landscape 3:2 image; bottle slightly right of center on plinth, medium close shot with full bottle visible; foliage frames the lower foreground and sides with generous warm background at top; balanced and beautifully art directed, physical objects occupy most of frame. Website uses this image in the right half of a split hero so keep main bottle comfortably inside the central 55 percent of the photograph.
Lighting/mood: warm direct sunlight from upper left, natural long gentle leaf shadows to lower right, subtle luminous amber glass, calm timeless earthy mood.
Color palette: warm ivory and cream, olive and deep forest green, dark amber brown.
Materials/textures: imperfect limestone pores and chips, matte tactile paper label, real glass highlights, delicate serrated leaf edges and veins.
Text (verbatim): label says "HERBAL SAINT" in small elegant dark serif capitals and "BOTANICAL FORMULA" in tiny capitals underneath; no other text.
Constraints: no text outside bottle label, no watermark, no people, no sci-fi elements, no space imagery, no neon, no decorative sparkles, no fake fog, no floating objects.
```

## Herb garden: final generation prompt

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: full-bleed landscape photographic section background for a premium botanical wellness website
Primary request: a lush intimate close-up of real fresh herbs growing in a beautiful garden.
Scene/backdrop: a living herb garden with overlapping dark forest green mint and melissa foliage, fine feathery chamomile foliage and scattered delicate white chamomile flowers with sunny golden yellow centers.
Style/medium: photorealistic natural botanical editorial photography, extraordinary tactile leaf texture and believable natural imperfections.
Composition/framing: wide landscape 3:2 image, close and immersive at foliage height, rich layers of leaves in foreground and midground, shallow but not excessive depth of field, no sky or horizon. Herb leaves occupy most of frame with white chamomile flowers emerging at intervals.
Lighting/mood: restrained dappled late afternoon sunlight illuminates a few leaves and flowers, deep soft green shade elsewhere; calm, sensuous and earthy, subtle film grain.
Color palette: sophisticated deep forest greens and olive highlights, warm white petals, small golden flower centers.
Constraints: no text, no branding, no people, no bottles, no fantastical plants, no sci-fi elements, no fog, no neon, no watermark. Natural photographic realism, not an illustration or render.
```
